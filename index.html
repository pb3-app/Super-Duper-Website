Challenge friends online

Sign up with a username, a picture and a 4-digit PIN. Everyone logged in shows in the online list on every page.

  1. Make an account

    Pick a name and a picture. You enter your PIN each time you log in.

  2. See who is online

    Players in a match show as busy, so you only challenge people who are free.

  3. Send a challenge

    Choose a game and its settings. They accept in one tap.

  4. Play and climb

    Every win and loss counts for that game and for all games together.

Your game record

A match against a friend adds a win or a loss to your record. Games against the computer go on a solo board of their own, so practice never costs you a loss.

Leaderboards for all 27 games

The overall board adds up every multiplayer match. Each game page keeps its own board and a solo board.

  • Overall board

    Wins and losses from every multiplayer game together.

  • Game boards

    Wins and losses in one game, on that game's page.

  • Solo boards

    Best scores, times and results against the computer.

  • Clicker total

    Your lifetime tungs, saved to your account.
