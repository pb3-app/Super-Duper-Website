Multiplayer with anyone online

Single player games never count. Multiplayer matches always do.

  1. Make an account

    Pick a username, a profile picture and a 4-digit PIN. You enter the PIN every time you log in.

    Step 1
  2. See who is online

    Everyone logged in appears in the online list, on every page.

    Step 2
  3. Send a challenge

    Choose a game and its settings. They get a popup and accept in one tap.

    Step 3
  4. Climb the leaderboard

    Wins and losses are counted for each game and for all games together.
