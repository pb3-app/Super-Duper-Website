🏏 Tung Cricket

Pick your team

A full limited-overs match: eleven a side, ten wickets, a toss, a batting order, a pitch and weather report, a powerplay, free hits, DRS reviews and a super over if it ends level. You set the field and choose the bowler and each delivery's line, length and type. When you bat you pick a shot and time it. Playing the computer never counts toward the leaderboard (solo wins and losses have their own board).
