🏈 Tung Tung Football

Play the computer

You play both sides: call the plays and run the offence, then switch to defence when the other team has the ball. Playing the computer never counts toward the leaderboard (solo wins and losses have their own board).

🎮 Controls

WASD or ↑←↓→Move the QB, the ball carrier or your defender
1–5 or Click a receiverOffence: throw to that receiver (the numbers float over their heads)
SpaceBall carrier: juke (dodge tackles). Defence: dive tackle. While a pass is in the air: go for the catch
Click in the airCatch or pick off the ball: the ball has to land inside the ring around the player. Click when the marker on the bar at the bottom of the screen is in the green middle (yellow either side is a weaker catch, red is a miss). After you throw, the receivers keep running their routes on their own and you stay with the QB until somebody catches it. You can only throw from behind the line of scrimmage.
E / ShiftDefence: switch to the defender closest to the ball

Offence: pick a play, then throw before the pass rush gets to you. Four downs to move 10 yards; on 4th down punt or kick a field goal. Defence: pick a coverage, then control one defender (ringed in yellow) to tackle, sack the QB or pick off the pass. A touchdown is worth 7. Quarters are short, and the clock only runs while the ball is live.
