🎨 Tung Tung Pictionary

Practice sketchpad: nothing here counts toward any leaderboard. Play Multiplayer to guess against friends.

🎮 Controls

Mouse / fingerPress and drag on the paper to draw
B E FPen, Eraser, Fill bucket
[ ]Thinner / thicker brush
Ctrl/⌘+ZUndo your last stroke
EnterSend a guess (type in the box)

How it plays: everyone takes turns drawing a secret word. Guess fast: the sooner you get it, the more points you earn, and the drawer scores for every guess. Letters are revealed as the clock runs down.
