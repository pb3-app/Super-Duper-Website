🎮 Controls

A ←Dodge left (dodges punches coming from your right)
D →Dodge right (dodges punches coming from your left)
S ↓Dodge down (Yellow Tung only, dodges both)
J Left clickLeft punch
K Right clickRight punch

Tap a punch for a quick 1-damage jab. Hold it to wind up a harder punch, then let go to throw. Watch the opponent's gloves: when one flies at you, dodge away from it. Dodge something and punch right away for a COUNTER: it's faster and does +2 damage, and whoever missed is off balance (can't dodge for a moment). Mashing punches makes you tired, so pause to reset. Throw your punch just as theirs is about to land for a PARRY (they're stunned for 1.5s). Getting hit while winding up resets your wind-up.
