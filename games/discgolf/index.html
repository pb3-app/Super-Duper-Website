🥏 Tung Disc Golf

Pick a course

18 holes, played in 3D. You throw Triple T ID cards instead of discs: a soft putter card, midranges, drivers, a card that turns over and a card that always hooks. Every card has flight numbers (speed, glide, turn, fade) like a real disc, and you have to time your power and your release. Fewest throws wins. Water and out of bounds cost a throw. Play a friend head to head or open a room for 3 to 4 players: everyone plays each hole at the same time, you see everybody else's cards fly and land (with name tags), and a scorecard shows between the holes. Your best total on each course goes on the solo leaderboard (log in to appear on it).

🎮 Controls

←→ or drag the viewTurn to aim. The dotted yellow line is the flight path at the power shown (and it moves live while the power meter swings). A shaky release and the wind can still push the real throw off it.
1 - 8Pick a card from your bag (the numbers are speed / glide / turn / fade).
QECurve the throw: anhyzer (turns right) or hyzer (hooks left).
Space ×2Press once to start the power meter and again to set the power. Then the release marker swings across the bar: press a third time with it in the green. A bad release sends the card off line, and the harder you throw the worse it is. Esc cancels.
SpacePutting (inside 10 m): you use the putter automatically. A ring wanders around the basket, and you press Space once when it is on the target. Farther putts wander more.
FSwitch between a backhand and a forehand throw (they curve opposite ways).

📱 Touch: drag the view to aim, use the sliders and tap the Throw button for each step of the meter. Goal: get the card into the chains of the basket in as few throws as you can. Trees knock it down, water and out of bounds cost a throw (and you throw again from where you were). Within 10 metres you putt: the power meter is replaced by a ring that drifts over the basket, and you press Space once when it lines up (it wanders more the farther out you are). Land within 3 metres of the basket and it is a gimme: the putt is given and counts as one more throw.
