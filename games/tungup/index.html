🧗 Tung Up!

⚔️ Challenge a racer (1 v 1)

Pick someone who is online. You both climb the same map; the first to the top wins. You choose the map and chaos level in the challenge window.

🏠 Open rooms (2 to 4 racers)

1. Pick a map

2. Pick your Tung

3. Pick your bat

4. Chaos level

Chaos adds natural disasters and silly events: earthquakes, meteors, lightning, wind, tornadoes, inverted controls, ink on the screen, blackouts, banana rain and more. Only Normal runs go on the solo time board.
