🏒 Tung Tung Air Hockey

Play the computer

Playing the computer never counts toward the leaderboard (solo wins and losses have their own board).

🏒 0 - 0🎯 First to 7

🎮 Controls

MouseMove your paddle (it stays on your half of the table)
WASD or ↑←↓→Move with the keyboard instead
EscBack to the menu (solo)

📱 Touch: drag your finger on the table. Goal: slide the puck into the other side's goal slot. First to the target score wins.
