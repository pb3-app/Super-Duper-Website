🎮 How to play

Click a cardPlay it if it matches the top card by colour or number / symbol. Wilds go on anything.
Click the draw pileNothing to play? Draw one. If it fits you may play it, or keep it and pass.

⊘ Skip: the next player loses their turn. ⇄ Reverse: the order flips (with 2 players it acts as a skip). +2: the next player draws 2 and loses their turn. Wild: pick the colour. Wild +4: pick the colour and the next player draws 4 and loses their turn. Play your last card to win. Online, the server deals and keeps every hand secret; if you take longer than 45 seconds the computer plays your turn.
