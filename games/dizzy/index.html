📱 Touch: tap or hold the screen (same-screen play: your own corner button). Your tung never stops spinning and every time you let go it flips direction. Bats bounce off walls once and your own bats can't hit you. Run over a crate or hit it with a bat: ⬆ grow (bigger, +1 health, slower), ⬇ shrink (smaller, faster), ◯ shield (blocks one hit), ✕2 double bats. Last tung standing wins.
