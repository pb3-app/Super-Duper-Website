🧱 Tung Walls

How to play

Be first to get your tung across the board to the far edge (the glowing row on top). On your turn you either step one square (click a glowing circle) or put down a wall to slow the other tung (click a gap between squares, or pick ▬ / ▮ first and then click; on a phone tap once to preview and again to place). You get 10 walls. A wall can never close off the last way through, and if the other tung is in your way you can hop over it. Your cosmetics from the Tung Shop show on your tung.
