🏀 Tung 2x2 Basketball

🎮 Controls

WASD or ↑←↓→Move your player
Space hold, releaseShoot - let go in the green zone of the meter
JPass to your teammate
KSteal / block
E or ShiftSwitch which player you control

📱 Touch: use the on-screen joystick and the Shoot / Pass / Steal / Switch buttons below the court.

Press Tip off

Move: WASD / arrows · Hold Space to charge a shot and let go in the green zone · J pass · K steal / block · E switch player · Beyond the arc is worth 3 · Each team is you + one computer teammate (2 on 2) · Tied at the buzzer = sudden death
