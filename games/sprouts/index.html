🌱 Tung Sprouts

Your turn: drag from a dot to another dot.

Press on a dot and drag to another dot, then let go: your line follows your path and is smoothed out. Drag back to the same dot to make a loop. The line stops if it would cross another line, so steer around. A new dot appears in the middle of every line, and a dot can only hold 3 lines.

How to play
  • Take turns joining two dots with a line: click one dot, then the other. The game draws the line for you. Each player’s lines have their own colour.
  • Clicking one dot twice joins it to itself with a loop. A line can’t cross any line or pass through a dot. If a line could go two ways round, click the one you want.
  • Every new line gets a new dot in its middle, automatically.
  • No dot may have more than 3 lines. Full dots turn grey and act as walls.
  • Normal play: if you can’t move on your turn, you lose. Misère: if you can’t move, you win.
