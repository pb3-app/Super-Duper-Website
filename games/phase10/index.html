🎮 How to play

DrawStart your turn by clicking the draw pile or the top of the discard pile (you can't take a Skip).
Lay downWhen your hand makes your phase, press Lay down phase (or select the exact cards first). You do it once per hand.
HitAfter you laid down, select a card and click a glowing group (any player's) to add it. On runs use the ◀ ▶ ends.
DiscardEnd your turn by discarding one card. A Skip makes the player you pick lose their next turn.

Phases: 1) 2 sets of 3 · 2) set of 3 + run of 4 · 3) set of 4 + run of 4 · 4) run of 7 · 5) run of 8 · 6) run of 9 · 7) 2 sets of 4 · 8) 7 cards of one colour · 9) set of 5 + set of 2 · 10) set of 5 + set of 3. A set is the same number, a run is consecutive numbers (1 to 12), and Wilds stand in for anything. Go out first to end the hand: everyone else scores their cards (1-9 = 5, 10-12 = 10, Skip = 15, Wild = 25). Only players who laid down move on to the next phase. First to finish the chosen number of phases wins (lowest score breaks a tie). Online, the server deals and keeps every hand secret; after 45 seconds of waiting the computer plays your turn.
