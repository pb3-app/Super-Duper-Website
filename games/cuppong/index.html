🥤 Tung Tung Cup Pong

Play the computer

Two throws a turn. Sink both and you get the balls back. First to clear the other side wins. Playing the computer never counts toward the leaderboard (solo wins and losses have their own board).

Your turn🔴 You 6 left🔵 Opponent 6 left⏱ 25

🎮 Controls

MouseMove the target ring to the cup you want
Hold clickCharge the throw. Let go when the marker is in the green
SpaceSame as click: hold to charge, release to throw
↑←↓→ or WASDNudge the target ring
EscBack to the menu (solo)

📱 Touch: drag to place the ring, keep your finger down while the bar charges, then lift to throw. Tip: the white circle around the ring shows how far your throw can stray. It is smallest when the marker is in the middle of the green. The ring also drifts side to side and forwards and back, so time your release for when it sits over a cup.
