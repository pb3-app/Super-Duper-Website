🌋 Floor Is Lava: Extreme

Survive the lava

The lava rises faster and faster. Stay on the furniture, bat the other Tungs into the lava and last longer than everybody else. Playing the computer never counts toward the leaderboard (solo wins and losses have their own board).

Round 10 - 0

🎮 Controls

AD or ←→Run (platforms are jump-through, so you can hop up from below)
W ↑ SpaceJump (hold for a higher jump)
J F or ClickSwing your bat: knock other Tungs towards the lava
E KUse the item you are holding
EscBack to the menu (solo)

📱 Touch: joystick plus the Jump, Bat and Item buttons. Goal: be the last Tung out of the lava. Touch the lava and you are gone.
Items glow on the platforms: 🚀 Rocket · 🦘 Spring shoes · 🛡️ Shield · 🏏 Mega bat · 🥤 Speed soda · 🪶 Feather fall · 🍌 Banana peel · 🦑 Ink bomb · 🙃 Invert zap.
Chaos events are announced a few seconds ahead: 🌋 Earthquake · ☄️ Meteor shower · ⚡ Lightning storm · 💨 Gale wind · 🌪️ Tornado · 🙃 Inverted controls · 🦑 Ink splats · 🌑 Blackout · 🎈 Low gravity · 🧊 Flash freeze · 🍌 Banana rain · 🏏 Giant bat sweep · 😵‍💫 Dizzy spell · 🔥 Lava surge · 🌋 Lava geysers · ☄️ Fireball rain. Crumbling, moving, icy and bouncy furniture makes it worse.
