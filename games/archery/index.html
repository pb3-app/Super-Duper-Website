🎮 Controls

MouseMove your sight over the target
Hold clickDraw the bow. Let go of the button to shoot
Space or ShiftHold your breath to steady the sight (it runs out)
↑←↓→ or WASDNudge the sight. Enter draws and shoots
EscBack to the menu (solo)

📱 Touch: drag on the target to aim, lift your finger to shoot, and hold the Hold breath button to steady. Tips: the wind pushes the arrow sideways, so aim into it. A half-drawn bow drops low. Draw fully, but not for too long, or your arms shake.
